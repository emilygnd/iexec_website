The problem

Public blockchains were built on transparency.
That's blocking institutional capital.

Three risks that block institutional adoption across DeFi and RWA.

01

Fund manager

When a fund moves millions into a yield strategy, the market front-runs the position before it settles.

02

Tokenized fund issuer

When a tokenized fund publishes investor allocations on-chain, confidentiality mandates are violated.

03

Corporate treasury

When a corporate treasury settles payroll, the amounts are permanently public.
